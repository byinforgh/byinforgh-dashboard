NOTE

From Level 1 onward, this is corporate-tier investment, priced for businesses with real operating complexity: multiple staff, multiple revenue lines, or multiple locations. It is intentionally priced above BYINFORGH's SME Ecosystem packages, because the scope of work — audits, custom builds, dashboards, staff training — is materially larger.
